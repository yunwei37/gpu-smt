Even under a favorable interpretation, the measured extra representation work and small supported paid set do not supply a credible complete-cost argument for porting this constructor. Reduced post-parse finalization did not remove representation cost; much of it moved into parsing.

## Decision and limits

Close this parse-fused/exact-canonical constructor without a GPU port or full matrix. Record a negative mechanism result, not proof that GPU checking is impossible or that the full research goal failed. It neither demonstrates the hoped-for recovery of the expensive capacity group nor supports an end-to-end speedup claim.

No GPU/full-matrix admission has been manufactured from this setup. Full CSLib/Mathlib device experiments, qualified body replacement, matched repeated strong-CPU comparison and complete service/SMT/native-state contributions remain unfulfilled by this route. Ordinary CPU compiling/sharing is prior art and is not a new systems contribution.

The fixed scientific goal and RQs remain. The next research decision must address a different load-bearing mechanism with actual paid opportunity, rather than another buffer/cache setting, count-only census, plain import reuse or narrower kernel.
