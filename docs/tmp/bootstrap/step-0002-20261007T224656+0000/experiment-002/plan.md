## Research question

RQ1: How much expensive native preparation can real verification candidate streams share across independent jobs?

Test whether preparation still accounts for consequential work on authentic failure-inclusive generated proof attempts, and how much an established immutable header root already captures. Prior52-context Mathlib proof/negative-control replay and retained SMT byte-prefix statistics cannot answer this cost question for actual generated attempts. The result directs the choice between deeper native preparation, remaining elaboration/checking and the dynamic GPU sibling; it does not license cold-versus-warm novelty.

## Paper-value admission

Role: supporting. Largest credible story unlocked: a source-grounded, cost-weighted account of preparation and residual work in real verification traffic, identifying whether there is room beyond established import/header reuse. Strongest reject argument: apparent preparation benefit is only cold imports on selected valid fixtures, while competent native roots already remove all useful repeatable work. Independent evidence: complete new generated attempts from two public test datasets, native inputs/responses including failures and exact source contexts, and costs at a matched native boundary. No released complete failure-inclusive Lean stream or retained measurement supplies those data.

Positive evidence keeps deeper preparation/branching as a candidate only if costly residual work can be shown shared; import savings alone route to the existing baseline. Contradictory evidence (little preparation or residual work entirely dominated by unique candidate execution) limits this mechanism on the selected policy/context. Mixed outcomes route by measured dataset/header/candidate strata, without success-only filtering. Invalid parity prevents a warm speed interpretation; retain that result and repair only the implicated transport/source boundary. Best alternative is immediate dynamic CPU/GPU checker profiling, but actual source construction and the remaining warm cost distribution are needed to choose its consequential work. This supporting experiment does not displace that sibling or supply OSDI novelty by itself.

## Expected and competing outcomes

Expected: official cold imports dominate many short rejected attempts, and compatible logical roots already capture much of that cost. Competing explanation: native source elaboration/tactic execution and failures dominate even when imports repeat; costly theorem preparation might remain, but this experiment does not infer its share from residual time alone. A small complete-boundary difference contradicts the import-dominance expectation for this cohort, not the entire Lean/SMT thesis. No hypothesis of uniform per-candidate cost or optimal scheduling is made.
