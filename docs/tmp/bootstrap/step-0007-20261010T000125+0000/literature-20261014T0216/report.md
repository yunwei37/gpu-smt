### Preparation mapping and memory

[PR15531](https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/pull/15531), observed Open, proposes sharing eligible repeated .olean mappings within one process instead of heap-copy/fixup. Its author reports import/REPL time and RSS observations on its pinned Linux revision; these are not our measured results or a merged guarantee. It is additional current same-mechanism pressure on generic prepared-graph or reference-count-sharing novelty. Preserve it as citation/source evidence; a numerical comparison requires a matched reproducible toolchain/workload and cannot be claimed from the proposal.

### GPU placement

[NVIDIA CUDA Best Practices](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-c-best-practices-guide/index.html#data-transfer-between-host-and-device) explicitly recommends minimizing host/device transfers, batching small copies and sufficient parallel work. Therefore negative-only host refusal, fewer transfers or ordinary batching are implementation practices, not standalone novel contributions. Native5 tests an admitted remaining-cost question on complete verification; its host_declined counter or compilation cannot establish useful GPU parallelism, accepted-proof coverage or whole speedup.
