# Root plan self-review

The new resource budget/watchdog are explicitly authorized by the current
user, overriding older48GiB-per-process collection instructions for new runs.
A client queue limit alone is insufficient because the actual warm pool
retains workers across headers. A two-live-worker semaphore must count
startup and retirement; limits must appear on real native /proc entries.
The independent parent monitor avoids service-loop logging starvation.

Both arms receive identical24GiB/48GiB accounting and instrumentation, forming
a fresh comparison point. Native successful proof computation is unchanged.
Watchdog-injected failures are a dependency test, excluded from timing claims.
A naturally untriggered48GiB monitor does not validate its kill branch;
exercise that exact branch at a safe lowered test-only threshold on real
inputs, verifying candidate identity and largest-REPL choice. No speculative
heap48allocation or co-tenant risk. Monitor sampling is not a hard RSS quota.

Retain prior partials and avoid replaying unsafe old queues. A raised safety
claim cannot be inferred from a process launch, a config field, or a mock.
Review is self-review under the user's explicit single-thread restriction.
