## Admission and paper decision
The1.70059x result is on arithmetic-selected Goedel sources. The complete
ProofNet component failed to improve a mostly rejected32-candidate stream.
Neither settles behavior on actual completed proofs from another prover.
Farkas evaluates the official DeepSeek-Prover-V2 miniF2F solutions. Testing
the current implementation against that existing strongest-method family on
the same proof corpus is more discriminating than more local tuning.

Positive evidence would extend the selected-Goedel frontend result to
another producer's proofs. A null or negative result would limit that
generality. Failure/response differences or non-engagement make the affected
comparison inconclusive. This still cannot establish native-state branching
novelty, a GPU speedup, a new external-certificate principle, all4RQ answers
or OSDI readiness. The underlying miniF2F problems overlap other evaluations;
these are different proof outputs, not a held-out independent domain.
